§ 02 — Problem Statement

Why Hinglish LID is hard

Language identification on code-mixed Hinglish text — Hindi and English (and Roman-script variants) interleaved in the same utterance. Standard LID tools assume clean monolingual input; social-media Hinglish breaks that assumption. The problem statement is fixed: classify language mix at the text level for downstream NLP in Indian-language contexts.

Research context

Corpus collected as part of an academic data-annotation project. Paper under review — full citation and scope TBD.

§ 04 — Preprocessing Pipeline

Dataset prep proposal

Run on the Spore platform — ingest, profile, filter, export. Pipeline below is the planned sequence.

Step 1

Two datasets — load both corpora, compare for redundant rows across sets.

Step 2

Intra-set duplicates — check and drop duplicate rows within each dataset.

Step 3

Empty rows — identify and drop null/blank text fields.

Step 4

Special characters — normalize or strip emojis, control chars, and encoding artifacts.

Step 5

Merge — combine into a single dataset if the two sets are mutually unique.

Step 6

Token distribution — plot row-level token counts, apply filtering thresholds as needed.

Step 7

Manual QA sample — read through a small sample to sanity-check data quality before training.
